Q & A

你可能想知道的。

什么是 AI 中转站？能用哪些模型？

AI 中转站提供统一的 API 接入入口，方便你管理 Key、查看用量。你的 Key 能调用哪些模型，取决于所属分组和权限。模型和价格可以在模型价格页面查看，实际可用列表以当前 Key 的 /v1/models 返回结果为准。

怎么计费？缓存怎么算？

按实际使用的 Token 收费，再乘以你所选分组的倍率。命中缓存的部分按折扣价计算，reasoning Token 按输出价格计算。充值按 1 元人民币兑换 1 美元额度入账。每笔用量和费用都能在控制台查看，也可以导出账单核对。

我的数据安全吗？

你的对话内容不会在平台上保存。

现在可以在线充值吗？

在线充值将支持支付宝，目前正在准备中。开放时间请留意控制台通知。

Q & A

A few things to know.

What is an AI API relay, and which models can I use?

An AI API relay gives you one place to connect, manage Keys, and track usage. The models you can call depend on your Key’s group and permissions. Browse the model pricing page for models and prices. Your Key’s /v1/models response is the final list of available models.

How does billing work, including caching?

You pay for the Tokens actually used, multiplied by your selected group’s rate multiplier. Cache hits are charged at a discounted rate, and reasoning Tokens are charged as output Tokens. Each RMB 1 top-up adds USD 1 in account credit. You can review every usage record and charge in the console, and export your bill to reconcile it.

Is my data safe?

Your conversations are not stored on the platform.

Can I top up online now?

Online top-ups with Alipay are being prepared. Check the console announcements for the launch date.
